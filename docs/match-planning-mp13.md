# MP13 — real career audit

Run `node tests/planning-career-audit.test.cjs` for two 32-week seeded careers. For a wider sample run `node tests/planning-career-audit.cjs 3,10,24,41 48 > mp13-report.json`.

The existing world soak uses synthetic match outcomes. This new opt-in callback follows the actual booked card, signed structure, authored promo or signing, vow reconciliation, live psychology and actual graph execution. The normal career settlement wrappers process real results. All four structures and five approaches rotate; no forced moves, opening windows or artificial professional memory. The existing world decision/booking policy and starting grant remain unchanged. The real-bout sample omits synthetic mouthpiece/corner extras rather than claim they were actually simulated. Fixed choice policy is not player preference. The paired live-psychology-off replay is not settled in the career; it is a local same-seed sensitivity comparison, not a matched whole-career counterfactual.

Report covers per-structure and approach results, consent, outcomes, stars, crowd, calls, witnessed objectives, sequences, prior professional experience, academy involvement, stage resumes, and matched live psychology differences. Hard checks cover legal choices, immutable signed layout and contracted finish, witnessed receipts, bounded values, career invariants and duplicate settlement. Low delivery, high injury or unbalanced ratings are descriptive findings, not thresholds calibrated to desired outcomes. Review actual output before any tuning; no production engine changes in this PR.

## First measured baseline (PR #462)

199 matches, two 32-week careers, 1.677 mean stars, 34.729 crowd, 199 booked outcomes, 535/796 witnessed objectives and 160/728 witnessed named calls. 50 live-psychology comparisons: +0.015 mean stars, +0.36 crowd, -0.02 ignored calls, no changed outcomes. No sequence was signed because the decision policy skipped the optional hearing and acceptance. The original dialogue count also incorrectly used the display name rather than speaker side. These two results were coverage defects, not evidence that the production systems were unused by real players.

The revised policy rotates sequence-first, counter-first and single-call decisions, reports offers/hearings/selections/signings separately, measures actual dialogue, and adds same-booking traditional planning comparisons (same seed, approach, participants and promo result; separately negotiated sheet). The latter isolates neither the structure nor the planning model individually; it is a local policy comparison. No production tuning yet.

## MP13F.7B — sequence-aware recovery pacing

Separate experimental live flag sequenceRecoveryPacingVersion (new structured matches only). Pending unattempted signed chains within 120 seconds of a possible start, including direct current prerequisite, are assessed using signed alternatives, base projected energy and a four-point buffer. Only the current controller with a deficit gets a preference, for an existing legal standing breather or grounded rest. Minimum willingness 35; no active chain/ready offer, pending attack, protection, manager pickup/crowd mode, closing 90 seconds, or heel structure. Actual landed rests impose a 40-second cooldown. Normal pool validation and seeded choices are authoritative. Final preference is bounded at .25–.60 times competing weight, no forced recovery or altered costs. Timing calculation is an eligibility bound, not a timing intervention. The career audit keeps main's settled career unchanged and compares separate same-sheet/same-seed paced replays, logging final validated recovery offers and actual landings. This replaces the redundant MP13F.7A comparison in routine CI (archived #473 results were identical across 141 pairs). Inspect completion, stamina/timing failures, injuries, outcome and crowd before merge; recovery landings are association, not proof of causation.

No changes to the signed agreement, stamina rules, timing thresholds or legacy matches. Records real selection opportunities where the primary fails the chain/runway checks but a signed alternative passes, including offers and credited actual openings. A zero opportunity count is a valid outcome; the focused fixture must prove the mechanism with actual authored graph moves. Timing-aware scheduling is separate MP13F.8B.

## MP13F.8B — timing-aware waiting preference

Independently versioned sequenceTimingSchedulingVersion for new structured live matches. Compute continuous earliest/latest safe entry for each signed variant from objective window, authored after thresholds, actual duration and finish runway. Only when an otherwise viable, currently legal signed opening is premature by up to 60 seconds, and no matching variant or other ready sequence is available, modestly reduce that opening's existing final pool weight (at most 45%). Require a legal low-cost state-preserving alternative already in the pool. No moves added or removed, no forced recovery, no altered thresholds, safety gates, defence, signed agreement or RNG calls. Exclude protected/manager-controlled matches and active attempts. A future preflight includes conservative routine energy loss; mixed stamina/grip failures are not timing targets. Audit actual final-pool adjustments and later witnessed openings/completions as associations, not guaranteed causation. Compare same signed sheets, seeds and manager decisions against MP13F.8A with pacing enabled. Reject absent meaningful improvement or adverse quality/safety. Old in-flight matches remain unchanged.
