# MP6C — variation and reviewed bank reuse

MP6B measured 79 live huddles, 316 lines and 38 direct bank reuses
with half the fixtures offline. MP6A found only 10/80 owners with
approved fragments. Broaden reuse by reviewing existing first sentences
from unconditional acknowledgements, independent calm-response
fragments and narrow context-free request leads. Neutral acknowledgements
may introduce a reservation; positive concessions never do. Source
owner, exact phrase, slot and allowed conditions are checked. No new
individual banks and no wholesale import of an on-air claim.

Composer v2 adds four shared syntactic frames for each request/reply/
resolution and two grounded reasons per topic. Concise voices prefer
shorter syntax. Prevent reuse of identical lead sentences in a huddle
where alternatives are available, in addition to tracking line IDs.
Use the chosen initiative's reason when the factual comparison supports
it. No new topic is invented and selection of moves does not change.

New rooms capture v2 identities and a composer flag. Existing v1 rooms
retain their old composer and subject policy for exact replay. Future
academy workers use the existing runtime generator and the same review
rules. Offline identity fallback remains available.

The test measures coverage and unique full lines on a controlled
all-roster fixture with all three response stances, then audits 80
paired real huddles with all banks present. These are diagnostic
metrics, not a claim every character has a unique voice or all topics
are equally likely. Cross-meeting repetition memory is deferred to MP8.
MP6D introduces actual multi-turn bargaining.
