# Phase 9I — Five authentic closing-route families

## Hypothesis
After Phase 9H (`9027d53`), the five plans still offer the **same general clean-finish setup invitation**, `rope_run` or `waistlock`, following ordinary wrestler-owned offence. However, Technical, Brawl, Spots and Safe have different source-authored paths and safety constraints. That generic offer risks collapsing realistic closing composition into a handful of repeated combinations.

The merged Phase 9H full 960-bout sample showed **24 credited sequences in 240 clean active bouts**, **13 genuine pressure→counter→setup→impact four-beat sequences**, **0 unearned late control losses** and **0 booked-finish route fallbacks**. By plan (48 clean active each): Story 4, Technical 5, Brawl 5, Spots 2, Safe 8. This is an audit of five different styles, **not a demand for equal scores**.

## Candidate correction
The actual wrestling graph already contains a large set of physically conditional authored moves. For the existing clean, winner-owned, in-ring standing-action setup opportunity, use an independently specified *plan route source set* instead of giving every plan `rope_run/waistlock`:

| Plan | Candidate setup vocabulary | Intended authentic approach |
|---|---|---|
| Story | rope run, waistlock, front facelock, strike exchange | visible momentum, counter, then finishing attack |
| Technical | waistlock, headlock, go-behind, wristlock | grip control, grounded takedown or suplex |
| Brawl | strike exchange, rope run, rebound, front facelock | strike pressure and heavy grounded hits |
| Spots | rope run, springboard rise, top climb, corner whip | legal rebound or aerial preparation and spectacular landing |
| Safe | headlock, waistlock, wristlock, front facelock | controlled low-cost landing; risky impacts explicitly excluded |

**No graph edges, attacks, probabilities of success, finish outcomes, or worker controls are created or guaranteed.** A candidate source enters the pool only if it is physically legal for the actual wrestler and the authored `A[source].next` contains at least one landing with a compatible required physical position, no defensive/counter/nearfall variant, and safe-plan limits. The same current-controller, clean signed Director-active protections remain in force. Existing non-route moves continue to compete. Unusual legacy saves without matching graph routes fall back to the old general invitations.

This replaces *only one* generic closing-offer branch. Genuine winner reversals, direct setup payoffs, Phase 9H second chances, earned nearfalls and Phase 9G counter attribution are unchanged.

## Verification
- Four structures × five plans checked for multiple authentic source routes, physically compatible authored outgoing impact, a live optional action, no RNG consumed by offered guidance.
- No winner privilege in dirty/metadata-only bouts; opposing wrestler still has authentic pressure/reversal decisions.
- Full 480 signed Director-active versus 480 seeded metadata control audit; each plan reports real credited setup→impact combinations, distinct sources and grounded impacts, complete four-beat routes, commentary bridges, late unearned-control losses, fallback, botches and average rating.
- Preserve the **zero unexplained late-control-loss** assertion. A regression means the candidate is not acceptable without further examination.
- No extra CI workflows. **No merge without user approval.**

## Interpretation
A different set of actual performer-selected routes is a better measure of stylistic variety than merely increasing aggregate runway counts. A plan with few safe legal options must not be forced into a spectacular or implausible finish to hit a numerical target.
