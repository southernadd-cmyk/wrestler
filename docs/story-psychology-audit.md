# Story wrestling: psychology and finish audit (read-only)

This first phase compares **actual performed** Story-arc psychology with mere graph labels, scheduled commentary and booked finishes. There are **no gameplay, score, UI or save changes**.

## What to count

**Real heat → hope → denial → comeback** must follow in time and each stage must contain an actually landed move by the correct worker. An arc name in \`g.history\` or a narrator's scripted sentence is **not** proof. Report both full sequences with and without the denial.

**Near falls** have different sources: \`scheduled_nearfall\` represents a bespoke, pre-scheduled *presentation*, distinct from a successfully performed graph pin, kick-out or roll-up. Record scheduled versus authored event counts, the time in the match, and the fraction of scheduled near falls with no grounded same-side landed action in the prior 30 seconds. This is a proximity proxy; it is not a pin-legality test. Only count \`M.nearfalls\` as the total presentation tally, not "earned near falls".

**Finishing** must separate a visited finish arc, a performed offensive action during the final 180 seconds, the last 60 seconds' actual offensive activity, the gap to the decisive bell, and the prebooked finish route. Do not pretend a scripted final route is an organically executed finisher.

**Skill and plan differentiation**: change ring, safety and psychology for real Story worker Sol Cruz, hold each opponent/seed/duration/finish/promoter/feudHeat constant across Story, Technical and Safe; 12 and 18 minutes. Avoid generic play style affecting the worker's native identity.

Do not fail an audit merely because the Story sequences or near-fall proximity are poor: those are precisely the questions the audit is designed to answer. Hard errors are impossible completed outcomes, impossible early scheduled nearfalls or inconsistent counters. A separate Phase 2 gameplay PR should target the largest measured psychological/nearfall deficit, preserve existing legal timing and counters, and compare identical cohorts before/after.
