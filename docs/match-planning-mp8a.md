# MP8A — execute negotiated sequences conditionally

Signed editorial plan v2 receives an independent live sequence ledger
when structured match execution is active. Plan v1 and metadata-only
bouts have no new state or RNG calls. A dramatic objective and a
specific signed chain are independent: other actions can satisfy the
objective but do not satisfy the chain.

A pending sequence offers the next real graph action only inside its
window, after objective dependencies settle, with matching controller,
position and down state. A running sequence also requires the most
recent graph event to be its exact prior landing. A 30-second continuity
limit, three-start limit and protected finishing runway bound attempts.
No offers when shortened, working hurt, or a big-spot request is pending.
The actual performer has an additional stamina check for expensive
reversals. Signed restrictions, honoured notes and safe-plan risk
checks apply before the normal candidate filter.

Add the offer to the existing weighted pool before all live validators.
A legal opening gets a soft preference; a legal continuation gets a
stronger but non-forced preference. All existing physics, cost,
attempt/defence resolution, RNG, finish route and outcome logic remain.
No artificial position, landing, stamina or control change.

At Graph.apply, capture the actual pre-state and prior event and
credit only exact expected actions with exact resulting state.
A different move, missed window or interruption cannot complete the
chain. Retry from a genuine opening up to three times; preserve the
best observed prefix separately. On expiry or the bell, incomplete
sequences are marked missed. MP8B will add richer adaptation reasons
and alternatives; MP8C will use delivery evidence in career memory.
No new scoring or psychology effects in MP8A.

The 80-room audit plays real signed bouts and compares JSON restores
against uninterrupted execution. It checks exact landed event evidence,
legacy no-op behavior, immutable plans, booked result identity,
interruption handling and full completion. Performance distributions
are measured in CI, not assumed.
