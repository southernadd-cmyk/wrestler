# Earned near falls: live scheduler cannot fabricate offence

The legacy timer fired a near fall at fixed fractions of the match, chose an attacker by alternation, and narrated a randomly selected impact **not contained in the move graph**, sometimes inventing a counter to hand control over. That made near falls exciting in commentary but disconnected from real wrestler actions, including the Story audit's 73.5% unprepared elite scheduled moments.

For the first staged release, **only the Story plan** changes its scheduled near-fall behaviour. Technical, Safe, Brawl and Spots retain their established scheduling and do not silently change their tested stamina/quality baselines. The same physically earned mechanism can be extended plan by plan after their separate A/B checks.

On the proposed Story live route, the next scheduled near-fall opportunity waits until a genuinely performed, landed ring knockdown by the **current controller** leaves the opponent physically down. A 30-second freshness and one-cover-per-landed-hit guard prevents a stale hit or two counts from the same action. Only then does the count occur, crediting the right performer and referencing the **actual performed move**. The opponent escapes as stipulated by the scripted near-fall — this is **not** the booked match finish. The action ledger records \`earned:true\`, \`sourceMove\`, \`sourceT\`, \`sourceSide\`; tests verify the exact prior event. The engine does not silently count a missed, blocked, countered or uncommitted move as a landing.

**Important constraints:** a scheduled opportunity can be deferred or missed entirely if the wrestlers never create the legal opening. The change should not grant a free takedown, move anyone from floor to ring, change a stipulated match's winner, or invalidate submission-only pin restrictions. Legacy explicit \`Graph.nearfall\` invocations remain available for backwards-compatible standalone test interfaces; normal ticks use only earned sources. Submission-only matches continue to use their existing escape presentation rather than a pin.

**Closing action continuity:** Story bouts in the final 90 seconds now favour a restricted list of existing, legal grounding attacks as the next cover opportunity approaches. During the final 60 seconds the match graph gets a real action-selection beat unless a previously committed attack, scheduled cover or special spot has already occupied that tick. Those actions are not scripted guarantees, and they must pass the original physical, stamina, weight, training and counter rules. The booked finish remains separately agreed and unchanged. This also guards against eliminating stale near falls by simply leaving an empty finishing stretch.

**Verification:** 576 paired genuine seeded matches, novice/competent/elite treatments, four opponents, 12/18 minutes and Story/Technical/Safe. Measure the source-verified scheduled count rate, its match frequency, offensive actions in the last minute, true ending and rating. Do not declare success if all near falls simply disappear; require a meaningful number of honest covers and stable stars. Keep finish-continuity changes separate to isolate any trade-offs.
