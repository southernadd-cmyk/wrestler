# MP8B — live follow-through and conditional adaptation

MP8A baseline: 78 live bouts, 59 started, 10 exact completions,
57 interrupted; no completions in Brawl or Spots. Save replay passed.

For newly created v2 bouts, persist execution policy 2. Previous
live saves retain their original policy, and legacy v1 remains a no-op.
A shared Sugeno willingness model reads signed consent, compliance,
both performers' professionalism, stamina and psychology. It sets
bounded preferences for legal moves, not permissions.

When a legal continuation is actually available, preserve its
physical state across the graph arc update and give the ordinary
move pool an opportunity instead of an unrelated scheduled spectacle
or narration-only tick. A seeded selection and attempt resolution
still determine what happens; the competing pool remains. The finish
runway, safety, location, timing, stamina, injury and manager-request
guards still apply.

If a connected intermediate move is unavailable, consider at most
one substitution per attempt. It must be a real positive-weight
graph bridge from the actual previous move to the next signed move,
with the same family, performer and exit state, no increased risk
and comparable exertion. The final named call is never substituted.
The actual legal landed move is recorded with the planned ID.
A completed substituted chain is adapted, not exact completion.
A different action, defence, lost state or timeout still breaks
continuity. Keep explicit interruption causes and best witnessed
prefix. No fabricated moves, new scoring, or career memory.

A paired-seed audit retains MP8A's prior-policy checks and runs
the same 78 signed bouts with the revised policy. Tests verify
actual graph evidence, substitute topology, final call, exact
restores, immutable signed plans and improvement over ten exact
completions. Adaptation incidence and per-plan completion are
reported for subsequent calibration; not assumed before CI.
