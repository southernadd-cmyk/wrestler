# Phase 9B — persistent selling and earned recovery

The Office now retains **actual focused damage** for both wrestlers in actively structured matches. After at least 3 witnessed damage points, a wrestler has to carry the consequence of a worked body part into subsequent offence, including comebacks. Relevant completed lifts, running and aerial moves incur a bounded 0.22–0.46 extra stamina cost and a modest candidate-weight reduction, without altering legality, agreed moves, outcomes or the star formula. Unrelated light actions stay unaffected.

A real recorded breath or low-cost rest reduces only this **extra** strain for 90 seconds; it never heals the focused damage. Commentary follows a performed strained action, refers to the correct wrestler and body part, and is capped at 3 lines per wrestler with a 110-second cooldown. Legacy and metadata-only behavior is unchanged, and old saves without selling state remain valid.

The optional saved `graph.selling` and post-match `matchSelling` are backed by actual graph-event evidence. Four-structure bilateral tests and the **existing** 960-bout A/B comparison check authenticity, finish integrity, quality and safety; no new workflows are added. Phase 9C remains a separate task.
