**Release date:** 19 November 2026  
**Code baseline:** Phase 10 merge, [PR #108](https://github.com/southernadd-cmyk/wrestler/pull/108); narrative-clock pacing repair [PR #116](https://github.com/southernadd-cmyk/wrestler/pull/116).  
**Status:** The v0.4.0 documentation and package bump are reviewed together; the code features below were already merged and deployed before this documentation release.

> The matches are scripted. The people aren't.

## What v0.4 means

v0.4.0 is the consolidation of the living 80-wrestler world and the Phase 0–10 match-graph / technical-school work. It is **not** a claim that every move has fully modelled grips, that the whole catalogue has expert-reviewed mechanics, or that a new machine-learning engine has been introduced.

The existing performance style (Story, Technical, Brawl, Spectacle), **technical background**, and **weekly career training** are three distinct systems. They must not be conflated:

- Performance style steers how a match is presented and what broad choices are inviting.
- The wrestler's assigned technical background changes the weights of **eligible, reviewed** techniques and authored handoffs. Of 80 assignments, 40 are provisional gameplay characterisations; they are not new canonical biographies.
- Weekly training, rehearsals and learnable moves are career systems; they do **not** yet permanently change the wrestler's technical school.

## What's shipped

1. **Graph Lab:** browser run sheet, actual move tape, searchable move atlas, pinned baseline comparisons and automated reproducible tests.
2. **Technical-school identity:** catch, judo, lucha, boxing, strength, acrobatics and restrained hybrid preferences; bounded legal move and authored-chain selection.
3. **Actual match depth:** original 336-move catalogue, original storyline arc graph, Phase 3/5/5b/8 curated legal transitions and Phase 9 authored follow-through combinations; moves remain subject to location, stamina, weight, risk and scheduled match time.
4. **Physical outcomes:** saved named aerial/springboard attempts and the four explicitly reviewed grapple attempts (vertical suplex, German suplex, sleeper, ankle lock); generated defence does not become an unrelated fake landing.
5. **Phase 10:** twelve named high spots trigger identifiable match-graph actions, with separate failed-attempt records. Match construction rewards witnessed links, body-part storytelling and earned comeback sequences; repeating the same move can be penalised.
6. **Match pacing:** narrative phases follow the booked clock rather than an accidentally reachable finishing arc. Manager-ordered shortened finishes remain legal.
7. **Rehearsal and continuing rivalries:** scripted match planning remains influenced by one-match rehearsal; repeated respectful rivalry promos acknowledge the later meetings rather than recycling the identical argument.

## Testing and release evidence

- The latest Phase 10 commit passed all **10** required PR CI checks, including full Node simulation, move validation, graph baselines, 500+500 match-quality comparisons, persisted rivalries and Chromium desktop/mobile journeys.
- The merged `main` commit passed its **seven** post-merge workflows, including GitHub Pages.
- Deterministic tests validate old-save compatibility and reproducibility *within the same engine version*. The same seed across different engine versions is **not** expected to produce identical move sequences.
- Passing CI establishes graph integrity and compatibility. It does not replace human judgement about convincing wrestling or nuanced promos.

Start with `npm test`, then [Graph Lab](../tools/graph-lab/) for sampled match tapes. Historical design notes are linked from [the documentation index](README.md); many describe the state **at the time of their phase**, not the current graph.

## Out of scope / next design review

**New post-release evidence:** The [500-bout technique and position audit](reports/v04-technique-position-500.md) is complete, with [reproducible JSON](reports/v04-technique-position-500.json). It observed 324 of 340 authored moves, zero violations of existing coarse timing/position gates, and major gaps in recorded grip and ringside-location setup. This audit **does not** implement new technique mechanics.

- Review the remaining **250** authored techniques rather than indiscriminately adding move names.
- Pilot grip-specific prerequisites, committed setup/counter paths and physically meaningful transitions, without inventing a full 3D contact simulator.
- Make school progression a separate future career-system decision, including save migration and player controls.
- Keep the [technical coverage and physical realism review](technique-and-realism-review.md) as the next development specification.
