# MP7C — signed conditional sequence commitments

New structured rooms opt in to sequence signing. On selecting a
discussed setup, retain the canonical entry and move IDs on the
negotiated objective. The editorial plan is version 2 only when a
conditional sequence is signed; all plans without one remain version 1.
The structure contract itself remains v1.

The sequence is a bounded JSON record with version, conditional mode,
explicit entry (control, position, down) and two to four graph actions.
The final move must be the negotiated named landmark and its performer
must match that call. No execution status, timestamps or landed moves
are recorded. The editor checks the selected offer against fresh room
eligibility and its original validated graph transitions, without
reapplying or re-inferring the agreed psychological reaction.

The signed-plan reader independently checks schema, topology,
position/control continuity, timing, exclusions and signed restrictions.
Participant fitness and actual grip, stamina and position remain live
checks; reading metadata is not proof of physical execution. Malformed
or unsupported plans fail the existing career activation boundary.

The review shows the connected setup and blocks signing if it becomes
unsafe or is corrupted. The manager can explicitly remove just the
setup, revise the call, or leave the objective open. A finish route
change alone is not a new move or a new negotiation: revalidate against
current conditions and retain the sequence only if still eligible.
No refund or duplicate award of the historic negotiation reaction.

The legacy caller and saved room default still sign only the final
named call. MP7C does not add runtime nodes, modify the director's
event-based achievement logic or force graph transitions. MP8 adds
runtime validation, execution, adaptation and delivery memory.

The paired 80-room audit covers signed v2 and legacy v1 comparison,
explicit removal, manual edits, later bans, tampered plans, restored
plan activation and unchanged live nodes/finish/consent.
