# Story hope choreography: physically performed, not just narrated

The read-only Story audit in #150 found: elite Story 18-minute matches visited hope arcs in 100% of bouts but only 25% had an actual credited, landed featured-wrestler action in hope, and just 20.8% completed a landed opponent pressure → featured hope → comeback chronology. Technical plans with that same featured Story wrestler achieved 45.8% complete chronology. 73.5% of scheduled near falls lacked a same-side offensive lead-in under the separate near-fall audit; **this PR does not change near falls**.

## Mechanism

The Story match director correctly keeps the antagonist in control during a heat-stage hope arc, but the normal pool often keeps selecting antagonist offence. The existing \`hope_elbows\` narration describes the *defender* escaping while its event is attributed to the attacker, so narrating a hope arc is not a real hope action.

Only after the featured Story wrestler's opponent has **actually performed pressure**, and only when the Story worker is ring-skilled and psychologically trained, the ordinary pool gives existing legal defender reversals a credible chance. That reverses control through the regular \`counter_strike\` graph action, **not** by the story director. When a properly recorded reversal occurs during the hope arc, preserve that physical control for **one** next 10-second beat and prefer the two already authored legal follow-ups. An ordinary chosen attack must then actually land to count as performed hope.

- No forced wrestler control on arc entry, fake mat rise, invented grappling, guaranteed counter, altered contractual finish, or free star score.
- No effect for untrained featured wrestlers or any non-Story plan.
- One authentic counter earns at most one immediate protected follow-through; timed arcs resume on the next tick.
- Stipulation, position, stamina, real counter/defence and pending-attempt guards remain intact.

## Acceptance

1. Demonstrate a real opponent pressure event, *selected* legal counter, and subsequent actually performed hero follow-through in one cohesive sequence. Control and phase/arc must be consistent in the event ledger.
2. 288 paired native Story bouts (three tiers, 12/18 min, four opponents, 12 seeds) directly report reversals and genuine hope actions.
3. Full 864-match comparison of original #150 vs this PR, matched identical seeds and skill tiers. Competent/elite 18-minute actual hope incidence must improve by ≥8 percentage points, actual full pressure/hope/comeback chronology by ≥5 points, and denial sequences not decline by more than 3 points. Other plans and novice hero unchanged; protect star ratings, late offence and nearfall counts.
4. Keep it draft/unmerged until independent Phase 9 quality and Node/Chromium safety are green and the actual results pass.

This is **Phase 2a: hope choreography only**. The nearfall system is the next independent audit/fix.
