# MP6D — bounded backstage bargaining

New structured UI rooms opt in to one additional exchange. Existing
rooms and previous test fixtures retain their original choice policy.
The manager may ask the other wrestler for a different proposal. This
does not commit a move, advance the scene, alter mood, or charge a cost.

The original board displays two cards per performer, so bargaining
uses a bounded extra shortlist from the same legal structure pool:
top-ranked, lower effort, familiar, finish-supporting and non-risky
moves. Eligibility continues to enforce same performer, same objective,
timing, physical legality, bans, compatibility and the booked finish.
The shortlist is not displayed as a larger board.

The partner's counteroffer is ranked by both fuzzy assessments,
balancing joint and weaker-side utility and giving a small preference
to factually distinct safety, effort, familiarity or finish benefits.
The requester responds to that actual offer. Reasons compare the two
alternatives, while mood evaluates the final proposed replacement
against the booked original. No fabricated agreement.

After the exchange, the original move, first alternative, second
alternative and open objective remain choices. Only the selected
decision affects mood and board. Stored dialogue is validated against
the frozen proposal before signing. No new loop or sequence execution.
