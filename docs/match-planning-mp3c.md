# MP3C — targeted calibration

MP3B job 139880795165 (PR #435): 6,977 observations, 800 signed
profiles, 200 replay checks, balanced booking coverage and 79/79
rules across ordinary and stress samples. No live policy yet.

Remaining correlations across owner means: flexibility/mentorship
0.984, assertiveness/protectiveness 0.974, caution/improvisation
0.942. Nearest-owner distance mean 1.685; within-owner mean 1.058.
These are descriptive of a low-fatigue roster fixture, not proof

Matched probes: fatigue 0 to 90 increased caution by 46.154 but
decreased improvisation by only 8.871. Prior adaptation changed
improvisation by mean +3.598, minimum -0.067: the positive cue could
reduce a skilled wrestler's estimate. Mentor goal mean +12.411;
prior humiliation +9.876.

Protectiveness is more responsive to the actual portrayal at stake,
rather than simply echoing ego. Mentorship now prioritises safe
guidance and explicit mentoring context over generic flexibility.
Fatigue has a more substantial effect on improvisation; evidence
of adaptation cannot depress the fully observed score. These are
provisional game-design changes, not empirical fits.

All 15 membership curves, 79 rule IDs, five other outputs and shared
kernel unchanged. No changes to the hard legality/consent constraints,
live negotiation policy or save format.

Test-only reconstruction of MP2C rules checks exact scores and traces
for unchanged outputs on 202 matched cases. The calibration checks
101 profiles, intended contextual direction, unrelated-output parity,
expert ceilings and minimum 15-point fatigue sensitivity. The existing
200 matched room stress fixtures enforce fatigue, adaptation, mentor
and humiliation responses. All existing audit gates remain.
